## Short Description

Turn full soccer games into organized player clips. Cut across multiple videos, label every action, review the strongest moments, and export locally or continue selected clips in KICKO.

## Full Description

KICKO TrimOut makes the hardest part of a player highlight simple: finding and organizing the right moments.

Import full games from your computer, cut every relevant action, and keep clips organized by soccer category. A multi-game project preserves the work as you move from one match to the next. Optional Scout Mode suggests a useful mix for the selected position, but you always decide what belongs in the final story.

When the selection is ready, download separate named clips, export one combined video, or continue selected clips in KICKO for player marking, tracking, effects, and final highlight creation.

Local editing is free. No TrimOut account or license key is required. Sending clips to KICKO is optional and requires an active eligible KICKO subscription. Original full-game videos remain on your computer; only clips you explicitly select are sent.

Included in one Windows installer: KICKO TrimOut, FFmpeg, FFprobe, and required media libraries. No separate codec pack, Python, or Node.js installation is needed.

## Customer Installation Text

1. Download `KICKO-TrimOut-Setup-1.2.0-x64.exe`.
2. Open the signed installer and confirm that the publisher is the expected KICKO TrimOut publisher.
3. Complete setup and launch KICKO TrimOut from the Start menu or desktop shortcut.
4. Import a game video and start cutting. No activation key is required.
